关于Na-Ga

Na-Ga is an artist who is employed as a graphic designer and illustrator by Key. Prior to working for Key, he was employed by Pearl Soft. Na-Ga has been working for Key since the production of Air as one of the computer graphic artists, but was able to majorly contribute to character design in Key's sixth visual novel Little Busters! with Itaru Hinoue, along with the later released Little Busters! Ecstasy and Kud Wafter.
