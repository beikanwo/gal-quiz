关于たにはら なつき

Tanihara Natsuki (born in Yamaguchi, August 18) is a Japanese character designer, mangaka, and illustrator currently living in Saitama Prefecture. She was previously affiliated with CIRCUS, then EDEN'S NOTES before going as a freelancer.

She is best known as designer and illustrator of various adult visual novels, including Circus' Da Capo II series, and often considered the artistic successor to Nanao Naru for the Da Capo franchise.
