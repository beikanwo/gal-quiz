关于影崎 由那

Kagesaki Yuna is a Japanese manga artist, born on March 3, 1973.

Her work has been published under three other names:
1) Kagezaki Yuna (same characters as her official name but different readings).
2) Kagesaki Yuuna, when drawing CG's for PC games.
3) Kageyama Yuta, when drawing hentai manga or H-CGs for PC games.
