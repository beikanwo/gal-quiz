关于なかむら たけし

Nakamura Takeshi, born 15th July, 1972, is a Japanese artist and illustrator from Kanagawa. He originally worked for IDES but later went to Aquaplus/Leaf.

He is sometimes credited as "中村 毅", an alternate spelling of his name, or as NAK村 (Nakmura).
