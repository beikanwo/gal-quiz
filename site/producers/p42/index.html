关于ニトロプラス

Nitroplus, sometimes written as Nitro+, is a Japanese visual novel developer that has developed a number of visual novels, including eroge. They also collaborated with TYPE-MOON to create the light novel series, Fate/Zero. Their works usually have dark themes such as reanimation of the dead and murder.
