关于戯画

Visual novel brand used by TGL and TGL subsidiary Entergram. It was used on both regular visual novels as well as games that feature mecha with action-oriented gameplay elements. One of its sub-brands, Baldrhead, has been used for several of these action-oriented visual novels. Aside from its visual novels, it was known for creating the Variable Geo fighting game series for PC9800, which was later ported to TurboGrafx-CD, PC, Super Famicom, Sega Saturn and PlayStation.

The brand was discontinued and ceased selling its titles on 31 March 2023.
