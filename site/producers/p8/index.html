关于キッド

KID (an acronym standing for Kindle Imagine Develop) was a Japan-based company specializing in porting and developing bishōjo games, founded 12 May 1988.

Bankruptcy proceedings commenced on 8 December 2006. However, in February 2007 it was announced that KID's intellectual properties had been acquired by the CyberFront Corporation, which would continue all unfinished projects until its closure in December 2013. Bankruptcy proceedings ended on 8 March 2007

Kaga Create then bought CyberFront Corporation and owned the rights to KID's works.

Publishing rights to the company's back catalogue are currently held by MAGES..
